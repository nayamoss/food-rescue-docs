Food Rescue HQ

Report it, match it, rescue it.
CodeTV Web Dev Challenge S3.E9
Naya Moss and Nikema

The problem

After conferences and events, good food gets thrown out because organizers have no time and no one to call. Nearby shelters need it. Food Rescue HQ connects the two before it is wasted.

What it is

A food waste management app for events. An organizer reports leftover food on a quick form. Nearby shelters and partner organizations get a call, claim what they can take, and confirm pickup. A live dashboard tracks where the food goes until it is rescued or the window closes.

Why this fits the brief

  • Real problem: event leftovers get thrown out
  • Works for shelters (phone calls) and event organizers (dashboard)
  • Tracks every portion until it is rescued
  • Twilio plus an integration partner
  • Built to delight: dramatic alerts, countdown, a celebration

How it works

1
Report

Organizer enters what food is left, where, and how long it stays good. One tap creates the incident.

2
Alert

Twilio calls nearby shelters and partner organizations. HQ goes urgent with a clock.

3
Claim

Partners press 1 to claim. Pickup is marked on the dashboard.

4
Rescued

Counters hit the total, the dashboard celebrates, or shows what was lost.

Event HQ (dashboard)

  • Countdown and state: urgent, rescuing, rescued, expired
  • Total, unclaimed, claimed and rescued portions
  • Partner list and live activity feed
  • Report panel with a quick form and confirm step
  • Demo controls: simulate a reply, fast-forward, reset
